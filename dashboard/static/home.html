The Road Ahead: Building Toward More Autonomous Robotics

THE BROADER GOAL
End to End Autonomy

Perception, Planning, Action & Verification in a Continuous Loop

Rather than treating manipulation as a fixed chain of commands, AERO is building toward a unified architecture that brings perception, planning, action, and verification into an unbroken feedback cycle. By exploring how learned models, explicit planning, geometric constraints, and feedback driven control reinforce one another, robotic systems can become truly adaptive, reliable, and capable of solving complex tasks.

01

Closed Loop Execution & Multi Camera Perception

Moving beyond fixed sequences to continuous real time perception. Overhead and wrist camera feeds eliminate occlusions, while active execution monitoring verifies grasp stability, detects deviations, and triggers dynamic replanning.

Multi Camera Perception Execution Monitoring Dynamic Replanning
02

Vision Language Action (VLA) Integration

Integrating pretrained VLA models (including Google VLA and OpenVLA) to bind visual observations and natural language to physical control, interfacing learned policies with inverse kinematics, joint constraints, and latency aware arbitration.

Google VLA & OpenVLA Kinematic Safety Bounds Action Arbitration
03

Expanding Simulation Scenarios

Evaluating AERO across complex assemblies, unfamiliar geometries, and unexpected failure modes. Simulation provides a controlled sandbox for measuring task completion, recovery behaviors, and policy consistency before physical deployment.

Complex Assemblies Failure Injection Hardware Transfer Bench

Core Engineering Foundations

Autonomous Vision Language Action

Unified cognitive reasoning engine. Ingests natural language commands and spatial memory to synthesize metric trajectories, high precision pick and place waypoints, and gesture actions with instant deterministic execution.

3D Semantic Spatial SLAM

Persistent spatial affordance memory tracking entity bounding volumes, contact relationships, grasp clearances, and dynamic tower layer states (Base, Mid, Apex).

Zero Impulse Kinematics

5 DOF analytical geometric IK with contact stabilization dwell, anti topple stationary jaw release, and dynamic transit altitude clamping (≤ 1.12m) to prevent toppling during multi layer stacking.

Tabletop Frontier Exploration

Autonomous multi view visual scanning across NW, NE, SW, and SE table quadrants at safe hover altitude (0.855m) to resolve occlusions and guarantee 100% table coverage.

60 FPS WebGL Studio

Monochromatic noir Three.js 3D viewport featuring an articulated Panda style cobot, OrbitControls, studio lighting, glowing TCP trajectory trails, and multi camera perspectives (Orbit & Top View).

Native ROS 2 Ecosystem

Complete ROS 2 package (vla_robot_arm) with cognitive thought publishers, action plan streams, semantic map nodes, and launch configurations for ROS 2 Humble/Jazzy.
